Why we exist

"Soko" is the Swahili word for market. We are taking the market online, so the people who grow food and the people who cook with it can deal with each other directly.

A farmer in Molo can grow an excellent harvest and still struggle to find a fair, reliable buyer. A restaurant in Nairobi can need the same produce every week and still not know whom to trust. Soko Digital puts the two in direct contact, and takes care of the parts that are hardest to trust: weighing, grading, delivery and payment.

Farmers set their own prices. Buyers know what they are getting. Money only moves when the job is done.

What we do

We do the work in the middle, so both sides can trade with confidence.

{% icon "badge-check" %}

Direct listings

Farmers list their harvest and choose their own price. We show the usual range for each produce as a guide.

{% icon "scale" %}

Weighing and grading

Produce is weighed and graded at the collection point, so what arrives matches what was ordered.

{% icon "truck" %}

Delivery

One delivery to the buyer's address in Nairobi, at KES {{ site.delivery }} per kilogram.

{% icon "lock" %}

Protected payment

The buyer's money is held until they confirm delivery. Then the farmer is paid.

What we believe

{% icon "hand-coins" %}

Fair prices

The farmer decides what their produce is worth. Nobody is told what to accept.

{% icon "clipboard-check" %}

Honest grading

Quality is checked before produce travels, and buyers can report a problem if it falls short.

{% icon "shield-check" %}

Safe payments

Money is held, not rushed. It moves only when the buyer says the order is right.

{% icon "smartphone" %}

Simple on a phone

Everything works on a basic smartphone and pays through M-Pesa, the way people already do business.

How we earn

We charge a {{ site.fee }}% fee on the farmer's price, and only when the farmer is paid. If an order does not complete, there is no fee. Buyers pay the price of the produce plus delivery, and nothing else.

Where we are today

We are in our pilot: one route from Molo and Nakuru to Nairobi, and {{ produce|length }} kinds of produce. Payments are simulated while we finish connecting live M-Pesa, so no real money moves yet. We would rather get one route right than promise everything at once.
