1. Using Soko Digital

Soko Digital is an online marketplace. Farmers list produce, businesses order it, and we collect, grade and deliver it. By creating an account or placing an order you agree to these terms.

2. Your account

Give accurate details, including a phone number that is yours for M-Pesa. Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

3. Listings (for farmers)

You set your own price. Make sure the produce, quantity, grade estimate and location you give are true. Bring the produce to the collection point so it can be weighed and graded. If it does not match the listing, the order may be changed or cancelled.

4. Orders and payment (for buyers)

You pay the price of the produce plus delivery (KES {{ site.delivery }} per kilogram). Your payment is held when you order and is released to the farmer only after you confirm delivery. At the moment payments are simulated while we finish connecting live M-Pesa, so no real money moves.

5. Our fee

We take a {{ site.fee }}% fee from the farmer's price, only when the farmer is paid. Buyers are not charged a fee on top of produce and delivery.

6. Problems and refunds

If an order is not right, the buyer can report a problem instead of confirming it. While a report is open nothing is paid out. Our team reviews it and either releases the payment to the farmer or refunds the buyer. Our decision on a reported order is final for that order.

7. Fair use

Do not post false listings, misuse the service, or try to avoid paying or being paid through the platform for orders that began here.

8. Changes

We may update these terms as the pilot grows. The date at the top shows when they last changed. If you keep using Soko Digital after a change, you accept the new terms.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms? Use the contact page.
