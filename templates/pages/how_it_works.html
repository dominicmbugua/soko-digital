  1. {% icon "shopping-basket" %}

    Browse and add to cart

    Look through the marketplace and add produce to your cart. You do not need an account to browse. Pick the kilograms you want from each farmer.

  2. {% icon "users" %}

    Register as a buyer and check out

    Give your delivery address, any notes for the driver, and the M-Pesa number that will pay. You can order from several farmers in one checkout.

  3. {% icon "lock" %}

    Pay by M-Pesa. Your money is held.

    One payment covers your whole cart. The money goes to a holding account. It is not sent to the farmer yet.

  4. {% icon "truck" %}

    We collect, grade and deliver

    Each farmer's produce is weighed and graded at the collection point, then delivered to you in Nairobi.

  5. {% icon "circle-check" %}

    Confirm delivery, or report a problem

    Check your order, then confirm it from your dashboard. Only then is the farmer paid. If something is wrong, report it instead.

  1. {% icon "users" %}

    Register as a farmer

    Sign up with your name and the M-Pesa number you want to be paid on. It is free to register.

  2. {% icon "clipboard-check" %}

    Post a listing

    Choose the produce, how many kilograms you have (at least 10), your grade estimate and your price per kilogram. We show the usual range so you can see where you stand.

  3. {% icon "package" %}

    Bring it to the collection point

    Your produce is weighed and graded before it travels. You can edit, pause or delete a listing any time from your dashboard.

  4. {% icon "truck" %}

    We deliver it

    When a buyer orders, we deliver your produce to them in Nairobi. You do not have to arrange transport.

  5. {% icon "hand-coins" %}

    Get paid

    Once the buyer confirms delivery, you receive your price minus our {{ site.fee }}% fee. Your dashboard shows what is paid and what is still on its way.

If something goes wrong

A buyer can report a problem with an order instead of confirming it. Nothing is paid out while it is open. Our team looks into it, then either releases the payment to the farmer or refunds the buyer. Read more in the FAQ or contact us.
