Set your own price per kilogram. We show the usual range so you can see where you stand. After you list, bring your produce to the collection point so it can be weighed and graded.

How your money moves

  1. The buyer pays
    By M-Pesa when ordering. The money goes to a holding account, not to us or the farmer.
  2. We collect and deliver
    Produce is weighed and graded at the collection point, then delivered.
  3. The buyer confirms
    They confirm the order, or report a problem with photos.
  4. The farmer is paid
    The farmer gets the price minus our 8% fee. A valid problem report reduces the payout.
