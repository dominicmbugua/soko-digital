How your money moves

Payment is held until the buyer confirms the delivery.

Read the full guide {% icon "arrow-right" %}
  1. {% icon "sprout" %}

    A farmer lists the harvest

    They set their own price per kilogram, with the usual range beside it.

  2. {% icon "smartphone" %}

    The buyer orders and pays

    By M-Pesa when ordering. The money goes to a holding account, not to us or the farmer.

  3. {% icon "truck" %}

    We collect, grade and deliver

    Produce is weighed and graded at the collection point, then delivered to Nairobi.

  4. {% icon "hand-coins" %}

    The buyer confirms, the farmer is paid

    The farmer gets the price minus our {{ site.fee }}% fee. If there is a problem, our team reviews it before any money is released.
